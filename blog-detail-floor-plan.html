Fast Floor Plan Drawing on Tablet for Interior Designers & Architects

Author: Esra ÖZDEL | Date: November 6, 2026 | Category: Architectural Drawing

During a client meeting or while on a construction site, you may want to instantly transfer a room's measurements (survey) into a digital environment. Previously, this process involved sketching roughly on paper and then finalizing it in desktop CAD programs upon returning to the office. However, thanks to mobile CAD solutions, you can now create a professional floor plan directly from your tablet while in the field.

In this article, we will examine step-by-step how to draw a room or floor plan from scratch, how to adjust wall thicknesses practically, and how to furnish the space instantly using TamgamCAD.

1. Creating the Basic Geometry (Outlines)

The first step is to define the basic boundaries of the space. Activating the Ortho Mode in the TamgamCAD interface will make it much easier for you to enter your exact measurements.

  • Line or Polyline: Select Polyline from the tool panel. Define a starting point on the screen.
  • Dimensioned Entry: After indicating the direction of the line with your finger (or tablet stylus), enter the exact length of the wall (e.g., 400 cm) in the dimension entry area at the bottom and confirm.
  • Return to the four corners of the room using the same method to create a closed area.

2. Determining Wall Thicknesses: Offset

It is a huge waste of time to redraw the outer/inner wall lines one by one after drawing a single line. Instead, use TamgamCAD's Offset tool.

Select the closed Polyline geometry you created, tap the Offset command, and enter the wall thickness value (e.g., 20 cm). The system will automatically create a parallel copy of your original drawing at the specified distance. Thus, the inner and outer wall lines of the room are determined in seconds.

3. Door and Window Openings: Trim

After creating the walls, you need to open spaces for doors and windows.

To do this, draw reference lines where the door or window will be placed. Then, select the Trim tool and tap on the wall lines remaining between the two reference lines. TamgamCAD will instantly prune those selected sections and open your door and window spaces cleanly.

4. Furnishing the Interior: Ready Furniture Library

One of the details that makes drawing a floor plan tedious is drawing elements such as beds, sofas, tables, and counters one by one. TamgamCAD offers a professional Ready Furniture Library to speed up this process.

  • Open the Library panel.
  • Select the furniture blocks you want to place (e.g., Double Bed, Sofa, Dining Table) from the list.
  • Drag the blocks to the desired area on the drawing screen. If necessary, use the Rotate or Scale tools to adapt the orientation and size of the blocks to your project.

Thanks to this feature, you can transform an empty plan into a fully furnished interior architectural plan ready to be presented to your client in seconds.

Conclusion: Design Independent of Time and Space

By applying these four steps on a tablet, you can create much cleaner, measured, and professional floor plans without the need for traditional paper-and-pencil notes. Specifically, the Offset, Trim, and Furniture Library tools complement each other, taking your design speed in a mobile environment to the highest level.
