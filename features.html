Advanced Drawing Tools

Discover our toolset designed to elevate your technical drawings to professional standards.

Ready Furniture Library

Furnish your spaces in seconds and detail your plans with comprehensive ready-to-use furniture blocks.

3D Viewing (Mobile Exclusive)

View your 2D technical drawings as a 3D model with a single click to instantly analyze spatial depth.

Advanced UX

A next-generation user interface that offers a smoother, dynamic, and highly professional design experience on mobile.

Insert Image

Add reference images (JPG, PNG) to your workspace for tracing or reference.

Import

Quickly import DXF files into your active project.

Export

Save your drawings in DXF, PDF, SVG, or PNG formats.

Line & Polyline

Create seamless vector paths and independent straight lines.

Smart Circle & Arc

Flawless curves using Center, Radius, or TTR (Tangent-Tangent-Radius) methods.

Rectangle & Polygon

Draw squares, rectangles, and regular polygons with quick dimension entry.

Trim & Extend

Prune intersecting objects or extend them to a specific target point.

Array

Automatically duplicate objects in circular or rectangular patterns.

Fillet & Chamfer

Round corners with a specific radius or create sharp chamfered edges.

Offset

Create parallel copies of objects at a specified distance.

Mirror

Create symmetrical mirror images of your selected objects.

Scale

Proportionally scale your drawn objects up or down.

Layer System

A hierarchical workspace with advanced layer visibility and locking controls.

Block Library

Turn frequently used drawings into blocks and call them instantly from the library.

Match Properties

Transfer the style and properties of one object to another with a single click.

Dimensioning

Add linear, angular, and diameter dimensions according to technical standards.

Hatching

Fill enclosed areas with concrete, brick, and various other patterns.

Text & Leader

Add explanatory notes and leader lines with arrows to point out specific details.

Professional Modes & Utility Tools

Isometric Drawing

Smart grid system locked to 30° and 150° for creating 3D perspectives in a 2D environment.

Boolean Operations

Perform mathematical operations on shapes: Union, Difference, or Intersection.

Tables & Settings

Add technical tables with customizable rows, columns, and cell content to your workspace.

Explode & Join

Break complex polylines into independent lines or combine open lines into a single continuous object.

Dark Theme Support

Professional interface option to reduce eye strain and provide comfort in low-light conditions.

Ortho Mode

Ensure precision by forcing drawings to only horizontal (0°, 180°) and vertical (90°, 270°) axes.

Keyboard Support

Comprehensive keyboard shortcuts providing instant one-key access to drawing and editing commands.

Filtering

Gain speed in complex projects by selecting only specific object types (e.g., Select Only Text).
