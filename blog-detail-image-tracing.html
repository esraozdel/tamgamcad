How to Do 2D Technical Drawing (Tracing) Over Reference Images (JPG/PNG)?

Author: Esra ÖZDEL | Date: November 6, 2026 | Category: Mobile CAD

You are in a meeting and the engineer or client across from you quickly sketched a draft on paper. Or you have an old machine part that needs to be manufactured but lacks a technical drawing. The best way to quickly convert such visual data into digital, millimeter-precise, and production-ready (DXF format) technical drawings is the Tracing method.

TamgamCAD, thanks to its "Insert Image" feature, allows you to take a JPG or PNG file from any of your devices and use it as a base (reference) in your workspace. Here are 4 steps to turn a sketch into a professional vector:

Step 1: Adding the Image to the Workspace

While on the TamgamCAD drawing screen, select the "Insert Image" feature located in the top menu or command line. Choose the sketch photo, scanned document, or product image you want to trace over from your device's gallery. The image will be placed on the drawing plane.

Step 2: Bringing the Image to the Correct Scale

Before drawing over the image, the visual must match real-world dimensions (Scaling). Determine a distance in the image whose measurement you know (For example, you know a certain edge is exactly 50 cm in reality).

  • Select the Scale command and mark the image.
  • Enlarge or reduce the image until it reaches its actual size. (If you wish, you can also draw a reference line and scale the image by aligning it with that line).

Step 3: Tracing Over with Vector Drawing Tools

The image is now ready as a base. To prevent your lines from blending in with the photo, open a new layer from TamgamCAD's Layer system and set the line color to a striking color like red or blue.

Then, using 2D drawing tools such as Polyline, Circle, Arc, and Line, start tracing over the geometries in the image. Thanks to TamgamCAD's magnifier tool and zoom capabilities, you can capture corners down to the millimeter.

Step 4: Layer Management and Exporting (DXF/SVG)

When you finish the tracing process, hide the layer containing the image you used as a base or delete the image. Only the clean and smooth vector drawing you created will remain on the screen.

By saving this drawing directly in DXF format, you can instantly transfer it to a CNC machine, laser cutter, or another engineering program. You can also export it in PDF or high-resolution SVG formats for presentations.
